Project Cost

The School Building Committee (SBC) selected massing study
C.5b, or Bloom, as their preferred design. The SBC submitted
this design to the Massachusetts School Building Authority
(MSBA) as part of the Preferred Schematic Report on December
9, 2024. On February 26, 2025, the MSBA approved the Town's
PSR submission, advancing the project in Module 4: Schematic
Design.

The estimated project cost of the Bloom design is $662

ion. This estimatation encompasses the school, a renovated
field house, and the inclusion of the Central Administration
Offices inside the building.

The project team recognizes how significant this project's price
tag is; they want to underscore the high cost of doing nothing.
